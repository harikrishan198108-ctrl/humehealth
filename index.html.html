Questions

Frequently asked questions.

What does Hume Pod measure?

Hume says the Pod tracks 45+ body-composition measurements and wellness indicators, including weight, body fat, muscle mass, hydration and visceral fat, depending on the device and app experience.

How does the Hume Pod work?

The Pod uses multiple-frequency sensors and segmental measurements. You step on and use the handles, then your results sync to the Hume Health app.

Can multiple people use one Hume Pod?

Hume's current product information says the device can recognize up to 24 different users.

Does Hume replace a doctor or medical test?

No. This landing page is for wellness and health tracking. Device measurements and app insights should not be treated as a diagnosis or a substitute for professional medical advice.

Is the Hume app free?

Hume offers a free app experience, while certain advanced analytics, reports and coaching features may require a premium subscription.
